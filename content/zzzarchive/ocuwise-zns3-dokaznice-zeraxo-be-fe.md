1. Delivered a clinically-meaningful diabetic retinopathy grading system that generalises beyond its training distribution. The final model attains a quadratic-weighted Cohen's κ of 0.8138 against ophthalmologist reference grading on IDRiD — an independent dataset acquired on a different fundus camera, in a different patient population, and never touched during development — versus 0.594 for the project's inherited baseline. On the referable-disease decision that governs real screening pathways (ICDR grade ≥ 2), the system achieves 89.8% sensitivity at 94.8% specificity, placing it within the inter-grader agreement band typically reported between human ophthalmologists (κ ≈ 0.80–0.85).
2. Established external validation as the binding model-selection criterion, having demonstrated empirically that internal test performance is nearly uninformative about deployment behaviour. Across seven candidate configurations, internal held-out κ spanned just 0.0225 while external κ spanned 0.1672 — a 7.4× wider dispersion. The configuration with the best internal score (0.8459) placed fourth externally (0.7934), and one configuration collapsed from 0.8294 internally to 0.6466 externally. This is a transferable methodological result with direct implications for how medical-AI systems are benchmarked and procured.
3. Engineered a leakage-controlled data pipeline over a harmonised 97,513-image multi-source corpus. EyePACS, APTOS and Messidor were reconciled onto a single ICDR 0–4 scale and partitioned using StratifiedGroupKFold at patient granularity. Audit of the naive row-wise split revealed 6,671 patients (82%) straddling the train/test boundary — both eyes of the same individual on opposite sides of the wall — compounded by a second, subtler leakage channel from the corpus's pre-computed geometric augmentations acting as near-duplicates. Both were eliminated; all reported figures post-date the correction.
4. Identified input spatial resolution as the dominant performance lever, ahead of architecture, pretraining corpus, or training duration. The defining lesion of earliest-stage disease — the microaneurysm, ~100 µm — subtends 1.9 px at 224² input and 3.7 px at 456². The signature was unambiguous: at 224² validation AUROC froze at 0.827 from epoch 2 and did not move across 30 epochs, whereas at 456² it reached 0.903 within two. A plateaued learning curve was thereby shown to be evidence about information absent from the input tensor, not about model capacity — a diagnostic heuristic now standing policy for the group.
5. Produced two rigorously documented negative results that redirect effort away from intuitive but unproductive approaches. (i) CORN conditional ordinal regression, the textbook-correct formulation for ordered categories, scored competitively internally (κ 0.8264) yet collapsed to 0.4886 externally and was rejected in favour of a simpler expected-severity decoding with validation-frozen thresholds. (ii) RETFound, a retinal foundation model pretrained on 1.6M images, was outperformed at matched resolution by an ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNet-B4 — its 224-native position embeddings and 16×16 patching being structurally ill-suited to lesions a few pixels across. Both findings are preserved in the repository rather than discarded.
6. Hardened the train–serve pipeline and eliminated three latent defects that had been silently corrupting reported metrics. Evaluation-time contrast normalisation was non-deterministic (a scalar clip_limit interpreted by the imaging library as a sampling range, randomising every evaluation pass); the production serving path bypassed contrast normalisation entirely via an unreachable branch, so deployed inputs did not match training inputs; and a checkpoint-selection key mismatch was silently discarding the best weights. None raised an exception and all passed the existing test suite. Serving preprocessing is now verified numerically identical to training to 2.4 × 10⁻⁷.
7. Implemented gradient-free, mathematically exact visual explainability suitable for clinical review. For the global-average-pool + linear head topology, class activation mapping is an exact decomposition of the logit rather than an approximation: logit_c = bias_c + mean_xy(Σ_k W[c,k]·A_k(x,y)), verified to reconstruct the network output to 1.1 × 10⁻⁶. Evidence maps are computed as a single tensor contraction with no backward pass, and are masked to the retinal field of view — an intervention adopted after measurement showed the single hottest pixel falling outside the imaged retina in 12 of 12 audited cases, an artefact that had been suppressing genuine retinal signal under max-normalisation.
8. Delivered a deployed, dependency-light demonstrator with full provenance and an explicit regulatory posture. The system is exported to ONNX and served with no deep-learning framework dependency (ONNX Runtime, ~3.2–4.8 s per assessment on commodity CPU, ~860 MB resident), behind systemd/nginx/TLS at a public endpoint, alongside 60 expert-graded reference images exposed through opaque identifiers so ground truth cannot be read from the network trace before inference. Every artefact ships a machine-readable model card recording provenance, frozen decoding thresholds, the selection rule, and quantified limitations — Mild-grade recall 0.16, Proliferative recall 0.31, expected calibration error ≈0.05. The system is positioned as clinical decision support for screening triage, not autonomous diagnosis, with the SaMD regulatory pathway acknowledged as a prerequisite to clinical use.
